Blast can affect rice leaves, collars, nodes, and panicle necks. Leaf blast can severely
damage seedlings, while node or neck infection can lead to few or no filled grains. A
general report of leaf spots is not enough to identify blast; ask about lesion shape and
colour, crop stage, whether the collar or neck is affected, and whether symptoms are
spreading.

Because several rice diseases and nutrient problems can resemble leaf damage, provide only
possibilities and recommend field confirmation by an agricultural instructor. Do not
prescribe fungicide products, concentrations, or application schedules.
