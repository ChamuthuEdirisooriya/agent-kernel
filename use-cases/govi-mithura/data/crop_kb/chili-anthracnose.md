Anthracnose can affect leaves, stems, shoots, flowers, and pods. Useful distinguishing signs
include small round sunken lesions that lengthen on pods, brown circular spots on ripe pods,
small dark spore structures arranged in rings, fruit rot, and dieback progressing downward
from shoot tips. These signs still require field confirmation.

Source-backed non-chemical measures include using healthy seed, removing infected plant
parts and crop residues from the field, rotating with non-host crops, improving drainage,
and maintaining recommended spacing. Do not provide fungicide products or dosages; refer
chemical treatment decisions to an agricultural instructor.
