Bacterial blight can cause seedling wilting and yellowing and drying of leaves. It can be
more severe in susceptible varieties and under excessive nitrogen. These broad symptoms
overlap with other diseases and field stresses, so ask about crop stage, where yellowing
begins, field distribution, recent weather, and fertilizer use before offering possibilities.

IRRI source-backed sanitation measures include removing weed hosts and managing rice
stubble, straw, ratoons, and volunteer seedlings that can harbour the bacteria. Variety and
treatment decisions should be confirmed with the local agricultural service; do not provide
chemical prescriptions.
